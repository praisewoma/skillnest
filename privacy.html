Privacy Policy

The short version: SkillNest does not ask you to create an account, and our learning tools process your answers in your browser rather than sending them to us. Our hosting provider may still process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Last updated: October 2026

No accounts

There is no registration, login, profile or subscription anywhere on this site. You never have to give us your name, email address or any other identifier to use the tools.

What happens to your answers

Your answers are processed by JavaScript inside your own browser. Typing keystrokes and quiz answers are used only to calculate the score you see on screen. They are not uploaded, not emailed, not written to a database and not shared with anyone.

Nothing saved between visits

The typing tools keep a small list of your attempts while the page is open so you can compare runs. That list lives only in the memory of the page you have open; reloading or closing the tab clears it. Test scores are not saved at all — if you want to keep one, write it down or take a screenshot.

Contacting us by email

If you choose to email us, we receive whatever you send, including your email address, and we use it only to reply. The contact form on this site builds a message in your own email app instead of sending it from our servers.

Hosting and server logs

This is a static website delivered by a hosting provider. Like any website host, that provider may record standard technical information in its own logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability. That processing is governed by the provider's own policy and is outside our control.

Children

The tools are suitable for learners of any age and deliberately avoid any request for personal information. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Questions

Ask anything about privacy on the contact page.

SkillNest uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, including which pages they visit and how they find the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, device and browser details, approximate location, and interactions with the website. It may use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information. This data helps us understand website traffic and improve our learning resources.

Google may process this information in accordance with its own privacy policy. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

SkillNest does not currently display advertisements. If advertising services are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.
