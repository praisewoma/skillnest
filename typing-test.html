How the typing score is calculated

The test uses the standard gross words-per-minute formula. A “word” is counted as five characters, including spaces and punctuation.

MeasureFormula
Words per minute (WPM)(characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed
Accuracy(correct characters ÷ characters typed) × 100
Correct characterscharacters that match the paragraph at the same position
Incorrect characterscharacters typed that do not match, including extra ones

The timer starts on your first keystroke and stops after 60 seconds, or earlier if you finish the whole paragraph.

Three tips for a better score

  • Accuracy first. Going back to fix a mistake costs more time than typing the next word carefully.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen. Looking at the keyboard is the single biggest limit on speed.
  • Stay relaxed. Loose wrists and shoulders let your hands keep a steady rhythm for the whole minute.
