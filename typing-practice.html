What each level means

LevelWhat you typeGood for
EasyShort sentences with everyday words and simple punctuation. Learning the key positions and building confidence.
MediumLonger sentences with commas and varied vocabulary. Building a steady rhythm across a full line.
HardSemicolons, apostrophes and longer, formal sentences. Accuracy work on punctuation and uncommon words.

WPM here is calculated the same way as in the typing test: (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Accuracy is (correct characters ÷ characters typed) × 100.
