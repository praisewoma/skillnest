How it works

Three steps, repeated as often as you like

1

Test

Take a short test to see where you are right now. The English, vocabulary and grammar tests take about five to ten minutes; the typing test takes one.

2

Practice

Work on what you missed. Every test has a practice mode that explains each answer immediately, and typing practice has easy, medium and hard paragraphs.

3

Improve

Retake the test after a few days. Questions and answer options are shuffled each time, so a better score means you learned the rule and not just the order.

Why SkillNest

What you can do here

  • Test your current ability A short, focused test tells you which areas are already strong and which need work.
  • Identify your mistakes Every finished test lists each question, your answer, the correct answer and why.
  • Practise your weak areas Switch any test into practice mode for instant feedback, or drill typing at your own level.
  • Retake tests as often as you like Questions and answers are reshuffled each attempt, so retesting stays meaningful.
  • Track your improvement The typing tools keep a small table of your attempts for the current visit, so you can compare accuracy and speed across runs.

SkillNest does not create accounts, so your scores are not saved after you close the page. Write down anything you want to keep.
