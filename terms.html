Terms of Use

Short, plain-language terms for using SkillNest's free practice tools.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Using the site

SkillNest is free to use for personal study and practice. You do not need an account and there is nothing to pay. Please use the tools in a way that keeps them available and pleasant for everyone else.

2. What you may do

  • Use the tests and practice tools for your own learning, in a classroom or with a study group.
  • Link to any page on this site from your own site, blog or classroom material.
  • Quote short extracts, such as a single question, with a link back to the page.

3. What you may not do

  • Copy and republish the question sets, paragraphs or page content as your own product, or sell them.
  • Scrape the site automatically in a way that disrupts it for other visitors.
  • Attempt to break, overload or interfere with the site or its hosting.
  • Present SkillNest's scores as an official qualification, or claim any endorsement that does not exist.

4. Our content

All questions, explanations, practice paragraphs and page text on SkillNest are written for this project and belong to it. The site's name, layout and code are ours as well. No copyrighted third-party text or images are used.

5. No warranty

The tools are provided “as is”, for practice and self-assessment. We work to keep the questions accurate, but we cannot promise the site is free of errors or always available. See the disclaimer for what our results do and do not mean.

6. Changes to the site

Tools, questions and pages may be added, changed or removed at any time, and these terms may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms? Use the contact page.
