Contact

Questions, bug reports, corrections and ideas are all welcome.

Email

The quickest way to reach us is by email: hello@your-domain.com

This is an independent project run by a small team, so we read everything but cannot promise a reply to every message.

Send a message

This form does not send anything itself. Pressing the button opens your own email app with the topic and message ready, so nothing passes through our servers. The form does not ask for your name or email address. If no email app opens, copy the text and email us directly.

Please do not include passwords, payment details or any other sensitive personal information.

Before you write

  • Reporting a wrong answer? Tell us the question and the option you think is correct. We read every correction.
  • Reporting a bug? Mention your device and browser (for example “Android, Chrome”) and what you expected to happen.
  • Privacy: we never ask for an account, a password or payment details. See the privacy policy.
